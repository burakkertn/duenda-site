Support

How can we help?

Send us your question, idea or a bug you found; we usually reply within a few days. You can also write from the app with Settings → Send feedback.

Email: destek@duenda.app

FAQ

How do I invite my partner?

Settings → Invite your partner → Create invite code. Send the code via WhatsApp or iMessage. Your partner taps I have a code in the app and enters it. Codes are valid for 24 hours.

What happens to my personal lists when we pair?

The person joining is asked whether to move their lists. Move puts them in the shared space; Don't move keeps them for 30 days, then deletes them.

What if I leave the couple?

Shared notes, movies and places stay with the other person. You get a new, empty personal space; your own to-dos come with you.

How does price tracking in the wishlist work?

Paste a Hepsiburada, Trendyol or Amazon (amazon.com.tr) product link and its name, price and image fill in by themselves. The price is checked once a day and its history shows on a chart. For links from other stores, you update the price yourself.

Can my partner change my library?

No. Your library is personal: your partner can see your books but can't change or delete them.

How do I report or block a friend?

Open their menu in your friends list and tap Report or Block. We review every report within 24 hours. You can unblock in Settings → Blocked people.

How do I change the color palette or language?

In Settings → Appearance you can pick one of three palettes (Night Tale, Scent of Earth, First Date), light/dark mode and the language.

How do I delete my account?

  1. Open Settings in the app.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm (accounts with a password ask for it; accounts created with Apple or Google just need a confirmation).

Your personal data is deleted immediately. What you wrote in a space shared with your partner stays there, shown as "Deleted user". If you can't access the app, email destek@duenda.app from your account's email address with the subject "Delete account"; we'll delete it within 30 days and let you know.
