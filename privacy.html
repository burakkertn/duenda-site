Privacy policy

Your data is yours

Duenda is an agenda for your notes, to-dos, wishlist, movies, books and places, on your own or with your partner. This page explains what data we keep and why, who we share it with, and how to delete it.

Last updated: November 28, 2026

In short

  • No ads, no tracking, we never sell your data.
  • We keep only what the app needs to work.
  • Your data is stored on servers in Finland (European Union).
  • Outside services get only the minimum they need: a product link for price tracking, the text you type for book and movie search. Never your name or email.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app with one tap.

1. Who we are

Duenda is provided by independent developer Burak Ertan. For any privacy question or request: destek@duenda.app

3. Sharing with your partner

In couple mode, notes, to-dos, reminders, income and expense entries, movies, places and game scores live in a shared space that both of you can see and edit. Each entry shows who added it.

Personal notes under "Mine" are visible to your partner but they can't change them. If you mark a personal note "hide from my partner", only you can see it. The same applies to your Notebook: your partner can see it, except items you mark "hide from my partner", and only you can change or delete them. Your partner also sees your profile icon, online status (you can hide it in Settings with "Hide my online status from my partner"), last-seen time and the names of your devices with notifications turned on. When pairing, you decide whether to move your personal data into the shared space. If you both agree, you can make personal notes fully private: you each see only your own "Mine" notes (the server never sends you your partner's personal notes). Turning this on or off needs both of you to agree.

Locked notes: If you lock a note, your partner receives it without its title or text; they only see "Locked note", can't open it and get no notification for it. You open it with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode; this check happens on your phone and no face or fingerprint data ever reaches us. Locked notes are not end-to-end encrypted: they're stored on our server like your other notes. Note order: The order you set by dragging notes is stored on the server and your partner sees the same order.

Library: your library is personal. Only you can change or delete the books you add; your partner can see your library but can't change it. If you like, you can hide your library from your partner with "Hide my library from my partner" in Settings → Sharing with my partner; while it's on, the server never sends your books to your partner and no notifications about books you add go to your partner.

Income & expenses and weight tracking: you decide in Settings → Sharing with my partner whether your partner sees the income/expense entries you add and your weight entries (including goal and height). When it's off, the server never sends these to your partner and no notifications about them go to your partner.

Period tracking: by default only you can see your entries. You decide whether your partner sees them in Settings → Period tracking → "Let my partner see it"; when it's off, the server never sends these entries to your partner. Friends can never see them.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the service, send notifications and emails, and keep your data in sync across your devices and with your partner (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c): performance of a contract).
  • For security, reviewing reports and fixing bugs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f): legitimate interest).
  • Period tracking entries are health data (GDPR Art. 9, KVKK Art. 6 special category data). We process them only with your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a), KVKK Art. 6(2)), which you give by turning period tracking on. You can withdraw it at any time by turning it off in Settings → Period tracking, and delete entries one by one or all of them by deleting your account. This data is used for nothing else and shared with no one (not even your partner unless you turn that on).

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell it.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers in Finland (European Union). If you use Duenda from outside the EU, your data is transferred there only to provide the service and only to the providers listed above.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, your data is kept.
  • When you delete your account, your personal data (profile, email, personal space and its content, the books in your library, sessions, device push tokens, notification history) is deleted immediately. What you wrote in a space shared with your partner stays there, shown as "Deleted user".
  • Personal data you chose not to move when pairing is kept for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Price-tracked product records (product link, name and price; they don't belong to any user) are deleted 30 days after they are no longer on anyone's wishlist.
  • Reports are permanently deleted from our server 2 years after they are made. If the reporting or reported account is deleted before then, the report is kept until the end of that period; the link to the account is then removed and only the reported person's display name at the time of the report remains. Blocks are deleted when either account is deleted.
  • Backups are kept for 30 days; deleted data leaves the backups by the end of that period at the latest.
  • Expired session tokens are deleted within 7 days.
  • Device push tokens are deleted when you sign out, turn notifications off, or Apple reports the token is no longer valid.
  • Email codes expire after 15 minutes.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and Turkey's KVKK) you can access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and object to its processing.

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete account. If you can't access the app, email destek@duenda.app from your account's email address.
  • Any other request: email the same address; we reply within 30 days.
  • If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact your local data protection authority.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed one-way. Each user and couple can only access their own space. Session tokens are stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain).

10. Children

Duenda is not intended for children under 13 (16 in the EU). If a date of birth is entered at sign-up, people under 13 can't create an account. If we learn that someone under that age has an account, we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date on this page and announce significant changes in the app.
