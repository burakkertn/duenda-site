Privacy policy

Your data is yours

Duenda is an agenda for your notes, to-dos, movies and places, on your own or with your partner. This page explains what data we keep and why, who we share it with, and how to delete it.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short

  • No ads, no tracking, we never sell your data.
  • We keep only what the app needs to work.
  • Your data is stored on servers in Finland (European Union).
  • You can delete your account from inside the app with one tap.

1. Who we are

Duenda is provided by independent developer Burak Ertan. For any privacy question or request: destek@duenda.app

3. Sharing with your partner

Personal notes under "Mine" are visible to your partner but they can't change them. If you mark a personal note "hide from my partner", only you can see it. The same applies to your clippings notebook: your partner can see it, except items you mark "hide from my partner", and only you can change or delete them. Your partner also sees your profile icon, online status, last-seen time and the names of your devices with notifications turned on. If you both agree, you can make personal notes fully private: you each see only your own "Mine" notes (the server never sends you your partner's personal notes). Turning this on or off needs both of you to agree.

In couple mode, notes, to-dos, movies and places live in a shared space that both of you can see and edit. Each entry shows who added it. When pairing, you decide whether to move your personal data into the shared space.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the service and keep your data in sync (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b): performance of a contract).
  • For security and fixing bugs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f): legitimate interest).

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling, and we don't sell it.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers in Finland (European Union). If you use Duenda from outside the EU, your data is transferred there only to provide the service and only to the providers listed above.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, your data is kept.
  • When you delete your account, your personal data (profile, email, personal space and its content, sessions, device push tokens, notification history) is deleted immediately. What you wrote in a space shared with your partner stays there, shown as "Deleted user".
  • Personal data you chose not to move when pairing is kept for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Backups are kept for 30 days; deleted data leaves the backups by the end of that period at the latest.
  • Expired session tokens are deleted within 7 days.
  • Device push tokens are deleted when you sign out, turn notifications off, or Apple reports the token is no longer valid.
  • Email codes expire after 15 minutes.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and Turkey's KVKK) you can access, correct, delete or get a copy of your data, and object to its processing.

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete account. If you can't access the app, email destek@duenda.app from your account's email address.
  • Any other request: email the same address; we reply within 30 days.
  • If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact your local data protection authority.

9. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed one-way. Each user and couple can only access their own space. Session tokens are stored in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain).

10. Children

Duenda is not intended for children under 13 (16 in the EU). If we learn that someone under that age has an account, we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date on this page and announce significant changes in the app.
